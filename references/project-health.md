# Project health

Assign health using evidence, not sentiment.

- Green: on track, no material blocker, next milestone credible.
- Yellow: recoverable risk, unresolved dependency, stale update, or milestone confidence below high.
- Red: committed date likely to miss, critical dependency blocked, approval overdue, or decision prevents progress.
- Unknown: insufficient or conflicting data.

Escalate one level when a committed item has no meaningful update for the configured stale-item threshold.
Do not downgrade a source-reported red status without direct evidence.
