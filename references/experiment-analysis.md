# Experiment analysis

For each experiment, retrieve and summarize:
1. Hypothesis and intended decision.
2. Population, allocation, start date, and sample size.
3. Primary metric and guardrails.
4. Absolute and relative effect with uncertainty.
5. Data quality or sample-ratio issues.
6. Practical significance and expected business impact.
7. Recommendation: continue, stop, ship, iterate, or investigate.

Use these labels:
- Directional: early movement, not decision-ready.
- Conclusive: uncertainty threshold met.
- Meaningful: effect is large enough to matter operationally or financially.
- Ready: instrumentation, guardrails, and decision criteria are satisfied.
