# Roadmap prioritization

Use a transparent score as decision support, not an automatic decision.

Default score:

`Priority Score = Expected Impact × Confidence × Strategic Alignment ÷ Effort`

Recommended normalized scales:
- Expected impact: 1-5
- Confidence: 0.2, 0.5, 0.8, or 1.0
- Strategic alignment: 1-3
- Effort: 1, 2, 3, 5, or 8

Then apply explicit judgment for regulatory commitments, existential risk, dependencies, sequencing, and executive commitments. Preserve the numeric score and the rationale for any manual override.
