# Deck workflow

Default 10-slide narrative:
1. Title and decision requested
2. Executive summary
3. Problem
4. Evidence and customer signal
5. Strategic insight
6. Proposed experience
7. How it works
8. Expected impact and measurement
9. Risks, dependencies, and rollout
10. Decision and next steps

Use the PRD as the content authority and designs as the visual authority. Label illustrative concepts. Keep one central message per slide.
